Background job backlog checklist

Use this before approving retry, replay, drain, delete, flush, or customer updates. Keep queue credentials, queue URLs, job payloads, tokens, customer IDs, user emails, raw logs, PII, and production data out of public tools.

1. State queue and backlog scope

  • Framework, queue name, job class/name, worker pool, backend, priority, and environment
  • Backlog count, oldest job age, throughput, latency, failure rate, retry count, and dead-letter or failed-job state
  • Customer-facing workflow affected: billing, email, import, export, webhook, reporting, support, or SLA

2. Check retry and cause context

  • Retry policy, backoff, jitter, attempts, timeout, visibility timeout, expiration, delay, or ETA semantics
  • External dependency, rate limit, database, memory, worker crash, deploy, scheduler, or payload-size cause
  • Idempotency, duplicate side effects, and stale-job handling before any replay

3. Define recovery and validation

  • Pause, drain, scale workers, retry selected jobs, replay safely, drop stale jobs, throttle, or contact provider
  • Queue depth, error-rate, Dead set, failed_jobs, dashboard, alert, smoke test, and next-update path
  • Named owner, reviewer, escalation path, comms owner, and update time

4. Avoid unsafe wording and private data

  • Avoid retry-all, flush/delete, silent discard, no-impact, or blame wording unless explicitly reviewed and approved.
  • Do not paste payloads, tokens, user emails, customer IDs, queue URLs, credentials, raw logs, PII, or production data.
  • Document customer impact honestly, even when recovery is already underway.
